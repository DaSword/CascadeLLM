# Benefits Enrollment

New hires have 30 days from their start date to enroll in benefits through the Workday portal. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following enrollment.

Akamai offers three medical plan tiers: Bronze (HDHP with HSA), Silver (PPO), and Gold (PPO with low deductible). The company contributes 80% of the employee premium and 60% of dependent premiums across all tiers.

Open enrollment occurs each year from November 1 through November 15. Outside of open enrollment, you may only change benefits if you experience a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, divorce, loss of other coverage). Qualifying events must be reported within 30 days.

Dental and vision are bundled together and entirely employer-paid for employees and their dependents. The 401(k) match is 100% up to 4% of salary, with immediate vesting.
