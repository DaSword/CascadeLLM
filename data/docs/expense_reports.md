# Expense Reports

All business expenses must be submitted through Expensify within 30 days of the transaction date. Expenses submitted after 30 days require VP-level approval and may be denied.

Itemized receipts are required for any expense over $25. Credit-card statements alone are not acceptable as proof of purchase. Photos of receipts taken in the Expensify mobile app are accepted.

Reimbursable expenses include: client meals (up to $75/person), domestic travel airfare in economy class, ground transportation, and conference registration fees. Alcohol is reimbursable only when included in a client meal.

Non-reimbursable expenses include personal entertainment, gym memberships, commuting costs, and any first-class or business-class airfare without prior VP approval.

Reimbursement is processed bi-weekly via direct deposit and typically arrives 7-10 business days after approval.
