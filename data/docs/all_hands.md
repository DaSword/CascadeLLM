# All-Hands Meetings

Akamai holds a company-wide all-hands meeting every two weeks on Thursdays at 10 AM Pacific. Attendance is expected for all employees. Recordings are posted to the #all-hands Slack channel within 24 hours for those who cannot attend live.

Each all-hands runs 45 minutes and covers: business updates from the CEO, a deep dive from one team (rotating), recognition shoutouts, and live Q&A. Submit questions in advance via the #all-hands-questions channel or anonymously through the Pigeonhole link.

Quarterly all-hands meetings run 90 minutes and include financial highlights, OKR reviews, and a town-hall-style open Q&A. These typically occur in the first week of each new quarter.

The annual company offsite replaces the all-hands during that week. Offsite locations rotate between SF, NYC, and one international destination per year.
