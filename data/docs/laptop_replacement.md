# Laptop Replacement

Akamai refreshes laptops every 3 years or when hardware fails. To request a replacement, file a ticket in #it-helpdesk with your current device serial number and reason for replacement.

Eligible employees can choose between the latest 14" or 16" MacBook Pro, or a Dell XPS 15 for those who prefer Linux. Custom configurations require manager approval.

If your laptop is broken, IT keeps a small loaner pool at the SF and NYC offices. Remote employees with broken devices will be shipped a replacement next-business-day; they must return the broken device within 7 days using the prepaid shipping label included.

Personal data on company devices: do not store personal files on your work laptop. When your laptop is returned, it is wiped immediately. Akamai is not responsible for personal data loss.
