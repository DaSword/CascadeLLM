# On-Call Rotation

Each engineering team runs a weekly on-call rotation. Shifts run Monday 10 AM to the following Monday 10 AM Pacific. PagerDuty handles paging and escalation.

Primary on-call carries the pager and acknowledges within 5 minutes during business hours and 15 minutes outside business hours. Secondary on-call covers if primary does not acknowledge within the SLA.

On-call compensation: $200 per weekday on-call, $400 per weekend day, paid out the following pay period. Time spent actively responding to incidents counts as worked time and may be banked as comp time.

If you cannot cover a shift, post in your team channel and arrange a swap. All swaps must be reflected in PagerDuty before the shift begins.
