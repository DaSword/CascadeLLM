# New Hire Onboarding

On your first day, report to the office at 9:30 AM or join the remote onboarding Zoom (link sent by your recruiter). You will receive your laptop, badge, and welcome packet during the first session.

Mandatory training to complete in your first week: security awareness (1 hour), code of conduct (30 minutes), data handling (45 minutes), and the platform overview tutorial (2 hours). All are available in Workday Learning.

Your manager will schedule a 30/60/90 day check-in plan during your first week. You will also be paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team to help you navigate Akamai culture and ask any "stupid questions."

Common first-week tasks: set up Okta, install required tools (Slack, Zoom, 1Password, GitHub Enterprise), join your team's standup, and complete your first PR (typically a documentation fix).
