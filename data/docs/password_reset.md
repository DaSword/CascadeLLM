# Password Reset

If you forgot your Okta password, click "Need help signing in?" on the Okta login page and select "Forgot password." A reset link will be sent to your Akamai email and require approval from your authenticator app.

If you've lost access to your authenticator app, contact IT helpdesk via the #it-helpdesk Slack channel or call the IT line at extension 4357. You will need to verify your identity through a video call with a manager or HR partner.

For all other password resets (GitHub, AWS console, internal tools), use the self-service reset flows. These are usually accessible via SSO with Okta and do not require IT involvement.

Password requirements: minimum 14 characters, must include at least one number and one symbol. Akamai strongly recommends using 1Password to generate and store unique passwords.
