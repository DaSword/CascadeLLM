# Parental Leave

Akamai offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all new parents, regardless of gender or how the child joined the family (birth, adoption, or surrogacy). This leave can be taken any time in the 12 months following the birth or placement of a child.

Birthing parents receive an additional 6 weeks of paid medical recovery leave, for a combined total of up to 22 weeks. Medical recovery leave must be taken consecutively starting at the date of birth.

To take parental leave, notify your manager and HR at least 30 days before your planned start date when possible. HR will work with you to file FMLA paperwork and coordinate with payroll.

Parental leave can be taken in two blocks within the first year. The first block must be at least 4 consecutive weeks.
