# PTO Policy

Akamai employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at a rate of 1.67 days per month, totaling 20 days per year. PTO begins accruing on your start date and is available for use after a 30-day waiting period.

Unused PTO rolls over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following calendar year. Any balance above 5 days as of December 31st is forfeited, so plan accordingly.

To request PTO, submit a request in Workday at least 7 days in advance for absences of 3 days or longer. Same-day or short-notice requests should be sent directly to your manager via Slack and confirmed by email.

Sick leave is tracked separately and does not count against PTO. Akamai offers 10 days of sick leave annually, which does not roll over.
