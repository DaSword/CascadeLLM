# Travel Reimbursement

Business travel must be booked through Navan (formerly TripActions). Bookings made outside Navan are not reimbursable except in emergencies with VP approval.

Per-diem allowances: $75/day for meals in domestic cities, $100/day for international cities, $125/day in NYC, SF, London, and Tokyo. Itemized receipts are required for any single meal exceeding $50.

Hotel rates should not exceed $300/night domestic or $400/night international. Above these limits requires manager approval before booking. Conference-rate exceptions are automatic.

Ground transportation: Uber/Lyft are reimbursable for business travel. Personal-car mileage reimburses at the IRS standard rate. Rental cars must be mid-size or smaller unless transporting equipment or 3+ travelers.
